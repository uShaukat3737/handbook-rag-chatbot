# Expenses and Business Travel

## Submitting expenses

Submit all expenses in **Ledgerline** within 30 days of spending the money. Expenses submitted later than 30 days need finance director approval and may be rejected.

Receipts are required for every expense over €25. Photos of receipts are accepted.

## Approval limits

- Up to €500: approved automatically if within policy.
- Over €500: requires your manager's approval in Ledgerline **before** you spend the money.
- Over €5,000: requires approval from the finance director.

## Flights

Book flights through the company travel tool, Skyroute. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or more, premium economy is allowed. Business class is never reimbursed unless the CEO approves it in writing.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is €180 per night. In capital cities the limit is €240 per night. Bookings above the limit need manager approval.

## Meals (per diem)

When travelling for work, you may claim a daily meal allowance:

- Domestic travel: €55 per day
- International travel: €75 per day

Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client dinners approved in advance by your manager.

## Ground transport

Public transport or taxis are reimbursed. If you use your own car, mileage is reimbursed at €0.30 per kilometre. Parking fines and traffic fines are never reimbursed.
