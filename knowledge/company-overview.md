# Brindlewick Robotics — Company Overview

## Who we are

Brindlewick Robotics designs autonomous sorting robots for small and mid-sized warehouses. Our flagship product, the Sorter-9, can handle up to 1,200 parcels per hour and is used by more than 180 customers across Europe.

The company was founded in 2017 in Port Halden by Marisol Ekwueme (CEO) and Tobias Rennick (CTO). As of this handbook's latest revision, Brindlewick employs 340 people.

## Offices

- **Port Halden (HQ):** Engineering, product, finance and people operations. Located at 14 Quayside Lane.
- **Kettering Bay:** Manufacturing, hardware testing and field support.
- **Remote:** About 30% of employees work fully remotely from approved countries.

## Our values

1. **Build it to last.** We design for ten-year hardware lifetimes, not product launches.
2. **Say the hard thing early.** Raise risks the day you see them, not the day before a deadline.
3. **Leave the warehouse better.** Every feature must make a warehouse worker's day safer or easier.
