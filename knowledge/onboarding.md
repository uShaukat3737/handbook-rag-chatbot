# Onboarding

## Before your first day

Your laptop and equipment are shipped to you 5 business days before your start date. You will receive your Peoplepath login by email the week before you start.

## Your first week

- **Day 1:** Welcome session with the People Team, laptop setup with IT, lunch with your team.
- **Day 2–3:** Security training (mandatory, about 2 hours) and product introduction to the Sorter-9.
- **Day 4–5:** Meet your onboarding buddy and start your first small task.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy is your go-to person for "how does this work here?" questions during your first 3 months.

## 30/60/90-day check-ins

Your manager holds structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. At each check-in you review your goals together and give feedback on the onboarding process.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks for both sides. After probation, the standard notice period is 2 months. Your manager confirms the end of probation in writing.

## Visiting Kettering Bay

All new employees, including software staff, spend one day at the Kettering Bay factory within their first 3 months to see the Sorter-9 being built and tested.
