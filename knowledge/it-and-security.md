# IT and Security

## Your equipment

Every employee gets a company laptop. Engineers can choose between a 16-inch MacBook Pro and a Linux workstation laptop; everyone else receives a 14-inch MacBook Air. Laptops are replaced every 4 years.

You may not install unapproved software that handles company data. Ask in #it-help if you are unsure.

## Passwords and accounts

- Use the company password manager, **Vaultkey**, for all work passwords.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on every company account.
- Never share your password, even with IT. IT staff will never ask for it.

## VPN

Connect to the company VPN, **Tunnelgate**, whenever you use public Wi-Fi (cafés, airports, hotels). The VPN is not required on your home network.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 2 hours by posting in #sec-alerts or calling the IT on-call number in Peoplepath. IT will wipe the device remotely. You will not be blamed for reporting quickly.

## Phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links. Use the "Report phishing" button in your mail client. Every quarter, security runs a simulated phishing test; people who click receive a short refresher training, not a penalty.

## Data classification

All company information has one of four levels:

1. **Public** — can be shared with anyone (e.g. marketing pages).
2. **Internal** — for employees only (e.g. this handbook).
3. **Confidential** — limited to specific teams (e.g. customer contracts, salaries).
4. **Restricted** — highest sensitivity (e.g. Sorter-9 firmware signing keys). Access requires security team approval and is logged.

Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into external AI tools or personal cloud storage.
