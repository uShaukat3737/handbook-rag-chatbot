# Performance and Growth

## Review cycles

Brindlewick holds two performance reviews per year, in **April** and **October**. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from two peers of your choice, and a conversation with your manager.

## Ratings

Reviews use a three-level scale:

- **Exceeds** — consistently goes beyond the expectations of the role.
- **Meets** — fully delivers what the role requires.
- **Developing** — not yet meeting all expectations; a support plan is agreed.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided only in the October cycle. Your manager nominates you, and a promotion committee reviews the case. Promotions take effect on 1 January.

## Salary reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year, also in the October cycle. Changes take effect on 1 January along with promotions.

## Internal transfers

After 12 months in your current role you may apply for open roles in other teams. You do not need your manager's permission to apply, but you should tell them before your final interview.

## Career levels

Each job family has levels from L1 (entry) to L6 (principal). Level descriptions are published in Peoplepath so you can see what is expected at the next level.
