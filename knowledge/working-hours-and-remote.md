# Working Hours and Remote Work

## Core hours

Brindlewick uses flexible hours. Everyone must be available during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 local office time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as you work your contracted hours (40 hours per week for full-time staff).

Kettering Bay manufacturing staff work fixed shifts instead of flexible hours. Shift schedules are published by the floor lead two weeks in advance.

## Hybrid work

Office-based employees work from the office at least two days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are **anchor days**, when teams meet in person. You may choose your other office days freely.

## Fully remote employees

Fully remote employees must live in an approved country (currently: Netherlands, Portugal, Ireland, Poland and Germany). Remote employees travel to Port Halden for the twice-yearly **All-Hands Week**, and Brindlewick pays for that travel.

## Working from abroad

Any employee may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. You need written approval from your manager and the People Team at least two weeks before you travel, because of tax and data-protection rules. Working from abroad for more than 30 days is not permitted.

## Home office setup

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of €400 for a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through Ledgerline within 60 days of your start date.
