# Code of Conduct

## Respect at work

Brindlewick does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind, whether in the office, at the factory, at company events or online. This applies to how we treat colleagues, customers, suppliers and visitors.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept gifts from customers or suppliers worth up to €50. Gifts worth more than €50 must be declined or declared to your manager, who decides whether you may keep them. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (such as vouchers) are never allowed.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any situation where your personal interests could conflict with Brindlewick's — for example, a family member working for a supplier, or a side business in robotics. Declare it in Peoplepath under "Declarations". Side jobs are allowed if they do not compete with Brindlewick and you declare them.

## Speaking up

If you see behaviour that breaks this code, report it. You can:

1. Talk to your manager or the People Team.
2. Use **Speak Up**, the anonymous ethics line, available in Peoplepath.

The Conduct Committee acknowledges every report within 2 business days and gives an initial response within 10 business days.

## No retaliation

Nobody will be punished for raising a concern in good faith, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation against someone who reported a concern is itself a serious breach of this code.
