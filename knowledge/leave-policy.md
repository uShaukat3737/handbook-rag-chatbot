# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays in their country of employment. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata.

Request leave in the HR portal, Peoplepath, at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

### Carrying over leave

You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, or they are lost. Unused days beyond 5 are not paid out.

## Recharge Week

The whole company closes for **Recharge Week**, the last week of December. Recharge Week does not count against your 27 days. Field support runs a small on-call rota during this week; on-call staff receive the same number of days off in January.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. You can self-certify sickness for up to 3 consecutive days. From the 4th consecutive day you need a doctor's note, uploaded to Peoplepath.

Sick leave is paid at full salary for up to 12 weeks in any rolling 12-month period.

## Parental leave

- **Primary caregivers:** 20 weeks at full pay.
- **Secondary caregivers:** 8 weeks at full pay, to be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

Parental leave applies equally to birth, adoption and surrogacy. Notify the People Team at least 8 weeks before your planned start date.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of a close family member, and 2 days for other relatives or close friends. No documentation is required.
