# Benefits and Wellbeing

## Health insurance

All permanent employees are covered by private health insurance through **Corvane Health**, starting on day one. Partners and children can be added at no extra cost. Dental is included; optical care is covered up to €250 per year.

## Pension

Brindlewick matches your pension contributions up to 6% of your base salary. Enrolment is automatic after your first month; you can opt out in Peoplepath.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of €1,500 per calendar year for courses, conferences, books and certifications. You also get 5 learning days per year that do not count as annual leave. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Wellbeing allowance

You receive €60 per month to spend on anything that supports your wellbeing: gym membership, sports clubs, therapy, meditation apps or massage. Claim it through Ledgerline.

## Bike leasing

Employees can lease a bike or e-bike through the company bike scheme. The cost is deducted from your gross salary over 36 months, which saves tax.

## Employee assistance programme

A free, confidential counselling service is available 24/7 to employees and their families. Up to 8 sessions per year are covered. The phone number is in Peoplepath under "Wellbeing".
